What to look at

01 · Consent copy

Leads with the why (relative position → directional voices) before the ask, and pins the spec's privacy promise — “never stored” — in its own visual pledge. The OK routes to the system permission flow.

Tone

Warm-black + amber: the vernacular of backlit audio hardware — VU meters, radio dials. Amber consistently means sound; cool slate consistently means silenced.

02 · Large type per spec

“Headphones on!” at display size, with L/R glyphs foreshadowing the stereo mechanic. One idea, one action.

03 · The hush cluster

The fader pill under each avatar — a channel fader pulled low — is both the affordance and the counter: tap to hush for an hour, tap again to undo; the number beside the finger is the total active hushes on that person. Your own pill (dashed, non-interactive) shows hushes on you.

Crowded neighbourhoods

The strip holds ~6 tiles without scrolling in portrait; beyond that it scrolls horizontally while the ruler keeps every pin visible, so the soundstage is always the complete picture. Try the demo toggle above.

Landscape

Rotation is a gift to this app: the soundstage widens (pan resolution improves), more tiles fit unscrolled, and vertical chrome compresses. Try the rotate toggle above.
